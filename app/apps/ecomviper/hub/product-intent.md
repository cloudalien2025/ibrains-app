# EcomViper Hub Product Intent

## Product Purpose

EcomViper Hub is the AI-native command center for canonical commerce intelligence and orchestration across marketplace channels.
Its primary purpose is to produce and operate on a trusted canonical product intelligence graph, then route actionable decisions to channel workflows.

## Target Operator

The target operator is a cross-channel commerce intelligence operator responsible for catalog quality, marketplace consistency, routing priorities, and trust-safe automation.

## Primary Workflows

1. Intake and inspect incoming channel feed snapshots
2. Match or create canonical product identities
3. Review merge quality and trust signals
4. Route prioritized actions to marketplace execution lanes
5. Monitor agentic visibility and recommendation outcomes
6. Close the feedback loop into channel apps

## Command-Center Philosophy

Hub should behave as a control plane, not a lane-specific editor.
The product should prioritize system clarity, queue orchestration, and decision support over direct marketplace form editing.

## Canonical Product Intelligence Graph Philosophy

Canonical entities are first-class.
Marketplace records are mapped artifacts attached to canonical products.
The graph should preserve evidence, confidence, provenance, and conflict state rather than flattening to one mutable listing table.

## Marketplace Feed Aggregation Purpose

Hub feed aggregation exists to normalize and reconcile marketplace-specific representations into canonical intelligence.
It is not designed to become a raw listing warehouse.

## AI-Native Commerce Intelligence Goals

- Produce structured intelligence signals for product quality, retrieval fitness, and trust
- Provide model-friendly context layers without losing operator auditability
- Enable AI-assisted triage and action ranking grounded in deterministic evidence

## Semantic Discovery Goals

- Track semantic coverage of product facts and intent language
- Highlight canonical and marketplace semantic gaps
- Improve answer-engine and retrieval readiness through structured diagnostics

## Agentic Discovery And Routing Goals

- Support agentic systems with canonical, trusted product context
- Route recommendations and actions to the right channel/system
- Distinguish diagnostics from executable actions with clear gates

## Shared Intelligence Goals Across Marketplace Apps

- Reuse canonical entities and shared signal contracts across Walmart/Shopify/eBay/Amazon
- Reduce duplication of scoring language and drift in metric meaning
- Enable consistent cross-channel review and prioritization

## Trust And Verification Goals

- Preserve source provenance and confidence for key attributes
- Track merge/audit events and decision history
- Provide explicit review workflows for risky or conflicting data

## What Hub Should Not Become

Hub should not become:

- a listing database clone
- a channel-specific publishing console replacement
- an affiliate/dropship catalog
- a consumer marketplace UI
- a monolithic execution engine without review boundaries

## MVP Product Thesis

A successful MVP for Hub is a canonical-first intelligence control plane that can:

- accept constrained feed inputs,
- produce canonical product mappings with confidence,
- surface prioritized routing decisions,
- and provide auditable, trust-aware operator workflows.

MVP value comes from reducing cross-channel ambiguity and decision latency, not from implementing every channel execution action inside Hub.
